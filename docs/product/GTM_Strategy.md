# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Feedback Nexus

## 1. Value Proposition
Turn the noise of customer feedback into clear product signals. Feedback Nexus bridges the gap between customer success and product engineering.

## 2. Target Market
- **Primary ICP:** B2B SaaS companies with 100-1000 employees. They typically have a dedicated Product Management team and a large Customer Success team.
- **Buyer Persona:** VP of Product or VP of Customer Success.
- **User Persona:** Product Managers.

## 3. Pricing Strategy
- **Seat-based pricing:**
  - $0/month for "Submitters" (CS team members who only submit feedback).
  - $49/month for "Editors" (PMs who triage and manage the board).
- **Justification:** We want to encourage maximum feedback submission without penalizing companies for having large support teams. We only monetize the power users extracting value.

## 4. Acquisition Channels
- **Integration Marketplaces:** The absolute highest priority is getting listed on the Zendesk Marketplace and Intercom App Store. This is high-intent, bottom-of-funnel traffic.
- **Content Marketing:** Publish templates on "How to run a feature request triage meeting."
- **Sales Led Growth:** Outbound sequences targeting "Director of Product Operations" titles on LinkedIn.

## 5. Launch Milestones
- **Month 1:** Launch private beta with 5 design partners (Acquired via network).
- **Month 2:** Zendesk App Store approval.
- **Month 3:** Intercom App Store approval + Product Hunt Launch.
